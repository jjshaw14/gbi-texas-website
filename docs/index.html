Who We Are

Austin's Electrical Contractor.

GBI Austin is the Texas electrical division of the GBI family of companies — a commercial and industrial electrical contractor serving the greater Austin market. We are led by Master Electricians who came up in this market, on these job sites, doing this work.

We have built here for more than 30 years. That is not a marketing number. It is the reason engineers, general contractors, and owners keep calling us back — through schedule pressure, design changes, and the kind of complex, live-facility work that leaves no room for error.

On a data center, a surgery suite, or a UPS cutover, the difference between a contractor and the right contractor is the crew standing on the floor. Ours have done it before.

We are not the biggest electrical contractor in Austin. We are the one that shows up the same way every time — and puts its name on the work.

The GBI Difference

Why Customers Keep Coming Back

We are a relationship-based contractor. Our work and our professionalism are what keep customers coming back — project after project, general contractor after general contractor.

Led by Master Electricians.

GBI Austin is run by people who started as apprentices in this market and earned their license doing the work. That perspective changes how a job gets bid, planned, and built.

Turnkey Capability.

We work hand-in-hand with engineers, general contractors, and architects — from design and engineering through complete installation and ongoing maintenance. One team, one point of accountability, fewer gaps for you to manage.

Depth on the Crew.

Our senior managers average 20-plus years. Our foremen and superintendents average 10-plus. Our field electricians average 5-plus. Experience is not a slogan here — it is who is standing on your job site.
