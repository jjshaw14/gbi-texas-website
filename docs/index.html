Who We Are

Austin's Commercial Electrical Contractor.

GBI Austin is the Austin branch of Great Basin Industrial, founded in 2015 by Travis O'Neal after more than 25 years in the electrical trade. In our first year alone, our team completed more than 100 projects for commercial clients across the Austin market.

Today, our Master Electricians bring a combined 100+ years of experience to every job, new construction, tenant finish-out, and critical facilities work, self-performed under one accountable GBI crew.

GBI strives to be the Austin market's preferred provider for every commercial and industrial electrical project we take on.

Safety. Quality. Experience. People. Integrity. Competitive. Six EPIC values our GB Nation lives by on every job site.

The GBI Difference

Why Austin Keeps Calling Us Back.
