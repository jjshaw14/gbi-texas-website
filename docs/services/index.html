Services Overview

One Contractor. Every Electrical Trade.

GBI Austin self-performs commercial electrical construction, new construction, critical facilities, and tenant finish-out, under one Master Electrician-led team with a single point of accountability.

What We Build

One Accountable Contractor. Full Electrical Scope.

A lot of electrical contractors specialize narrowly, new construction only, or finish-out only. GBI Austin self-performs across the full commercial electrical scope: ground-up shell buildings, tenant finish-out, and critical facilities work, so there's no gap between who built it and who finishes it.

New Construction

Shell buildings, tilt-wall construction, data centers, auto dealerships, and free-standing medical buildings. Complete electrical site packages from the ground up.

Critical Facilities & Data Centers

PDUs, UPS system upgrades, generator system replacements, and power for surgery centers, imaging centers, and technology labs.

Self-Perform Advantage

Why One Electrical Contractor Matters.

When the same crew handles new construction, tenant finish-out, and critical facilities work, there's no gap between scopes and no finger-pointing on schedule. GBI Austin's estimators worked the field first, so every bid reflects what's actually practical to install, not just what the drawings show.

‘We pride ourselves on the quality of our work and the repeat business we consistently earn.’ That's not a slogan. It's a standard we hold ourselves to on every job.
