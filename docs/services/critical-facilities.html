Services / Critical Facilities

Where Downtime Is Not an Option.

Some facilities cannot go dark. Data centers, hospitals, imaging suites, and technology labs demand electrical work planned to the minute and executed without error — often in live, occupied environments. This is where GBI Austin’s experience matters most.

Capabilities

What We Build & Maintain

  • Data centers
  • UPS system upgrades
  • Generator system replacements
  • Surgery and imaging centers
  • Technology labs
Approach

How We Work.

Critical-facility work is planned work. We sequence cutovers, coordinate shutdowns, and build in the redundancy and documentation these environments require — so the mission-critical load never sees the difference. Hospital-grade installations, standby power, and turnkey packages including data raceways and power to every piece of equipment.

In a live facility, the plan is the product. We build the plan before we build the job.
