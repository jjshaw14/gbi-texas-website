Informatica · Austin, Texas
Commercial ConstructionGround-Up + High-End Finish-Out

The Project. A 95,000-square-foot ground-up building with a 35,000-square-foot high-end tenant finish-out.

Our Scope. Complete electrical site package, intricate recessed exterior and interior lighting, and elevator shaft power and raceways — plus a high-end finish featuring custom light fixtures, advanced lighting controls, and a wire-basket tray system carrying low-voltage conductors through exposed-to-deck ceilings.

The Result. A finished space where the electrical work matches the architecture.
