Clean Scapes

Clean Scapes · Austin, Texas
New Construction

A 23,000 SF ground-up headquarters building, combining office and warehouse finish-out under one complete electrical site package.

GBI Austin delivered the complete electrical site package for Clean Scapes' new Austin headquarters, a 23,000 square foot ground-up building combining an 8,000 square foot office finish-out with a 15,000 square foot warehouse finish-out.

Scope included power to the site's lift station and a recessed and suspended light fixture package throughout the finished office and warehouse spaces.
