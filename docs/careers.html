Careers

Join a Team Built by Electricians.

We’re always looking for electricians, foremen, and project managers who take pride in the work. If you’ve done it right and want to keep getting better, we want to hear from you.

The Honest Truth About Working at GBI Austin

What to Expect.

Commercial electrical construction is demanding work. The hours are long, the conditions are real, and the standard doesn't move for anyone. We're not going to tell you otherwise.

What we can tell you is this: GBI Austin is run by Master Electricians who started exactly where you're starting. If you do good work for us, we'll do good work for you, and there's a real path from apprentice to journeyman to Master Electrician here.

The Culture

Great Basin Nation.

Great Basin Nation is what this company actually is, a name employees gave it, not leadership. Field and office, Utah and Texas, everyone who wears this logo is on the same team.

We're a drug-free workplace with above-average pay for above-average performers, and we take care of our own.

Now Hiring

Apprentice Electricians.

Full-time, permanent work across the Austin area. Commercial electrical experience preferred.

What We're Looking For

A valid TDLR electrician license, your own hand tools, reliable transportation, and the ability to pass a drug and background screen.

What You Get

Competitive wage, health/dental/vision/life insurance, paid vacation and holidays, 401(k), and IEC apprenticeship sponsorship after 6 months in good standing.

Where We Hire

Roles Across the Austin Market.

GBI Austin hires electricians, foremen, and project managers for commercial electrical work throughout the Austin area, as part of the wider Great Basin Industrial network across the western United States.

GBI Austin is a drug-free workplace; all applicants must pass a pre-employment drug test. GBI pays above-average wages for above-average performers. As a member of the CenTex IEC, apprenticeship school sponsorship may be offered to team members who have completed six months of employment in good standing.

We are an Equal Opportunity Employer. Minorities, veterans, and women strongly encouraged to apply.
