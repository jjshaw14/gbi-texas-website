About / Experience

Over 30 Years. One Market.

GBI Austin has served the commercial electrical construction needs of the Austin market for more than 30 years. Our management team is led by Master Electricians with over 100 combined years of experience — all of it here. We know this market, its inspectors, its general contractors, and its buildings.

Product Expertise

GBI Austin covers the full range of electrical construction — commercial to industrial, data centers to tenant improvements. Whatever the building calls for, we have built it before.

Turnkey Services

We work closely with engineers, general contractors, and architects to deliver turnkey solutions: design and engineering, electrical maintenance and repairs, and complete installations — start to finish, under one team.

Industry Knowledge

Our seasoned crews have a track record of completing projects across multiple electrical sectors for many of the world’s leading companies and general contractors.
