About / Our Story

Built by Electricians.

GBI Austin was not built in a boardroom. It was built in the field — by electricians who have run the pipe, pulled the wire, set the gear, and stood behind the work when the inspector showed up.

Where It Started

GBI Austin was founded in 2015 by Travis O’Neal, a Master Electrician with more than 25 years in the Austin market. He built the company the way he learned the trade — one relationship, one job, one crew at a time. GBI completed more than 100 projects in its first year, and has grown steadily since on the same foundation: do good work, tell the truth, and earn the next job.

The experience behind GBI Austin runs longer than the company itself. Our management team has worked and grown together in this market for more than 30 years, with over 100 combined years of Master Electrician experience — all of it here, in Austin.

We are a relationship-based contractor. The work and the professionalism are what keep customers coming back. Everything else follows from that.

Earned, Not Assumed

Every capability GBI Austin has today was earned the same way: a customer trusted us with a job, we delivered, and they asked us to take on more. Shell and site power led to tenant finish-outs. Finish-outs led to critical facilities and data centers. We did not diversify because a plan said to — we grew because customers asked, and we proved we could.

What We Are Today

Today GBI Austin delivers commercial and industrial electrical construction across the Austin market — from ground-up new construction to high-end finish-outs to live critical-facility work. As the Texas electrical division of the GBI family of companies, we bring the discipline, safety culture, and quality systems of a national industrial builder to the local market, with a team that has never worked anywhere else.

Built by Electricians

What That Means

The people who run GBI Austin have run crews. They have been on the tools. They have solved real problems on live job sites with the schedule on the line. That background is not incidental — it is the source of how this company operates. Leaders who have done the work make better decisions, hold a higher standard, and build the next generation of electricians by example.
