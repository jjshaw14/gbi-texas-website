Apprentice Electricians.

GBI Austin is hiring apprentice electricians with commercial experience for full-time, permanent work across the Austin area.

  • Your own hand tools
  • Reliable transportation
  • Ability to pass a drug and background screen

What you get:

  • Competitive wage
  • Health, dental, vision, and life insurance

  • 401(k)
